`knowledge-samples/*.html` is fabricated for demonstration
(`示例客户A`, `MOCK-CUST-0001`, `MOCK-ORD-*`, `MOCK-DEVICE-A07`, `CHG-*`).

credentials, no phone numbers, no emails and no production IP addresses**.
